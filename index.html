As easy as buying electricity tokens.

Follow one KSh 500 top-up from a tenant's phone to your account.

  1. Step 1 of 5

    Wanjiru tops up KSh 500 on M-Pesa.

    She uses the paybill number on her meter or the Smarta app, then enters her PIN like any other payment.

  2. Step 2 of 5

    She gets a water token.

    It's a 20-digit number, just like an electricity token. It only works on her meter, and only once.

  3. Step 3 of 5

    Her meter loads it by itself.

    The token travels to the meter over the mobile network. She also gets it by SMS, so she can type it in if the signal is weak.

  4. Step 4 of 5

    Water flows, and credit counts down.

    When it runs low she gets an SMS with time to top up. You never have to send a reminder.

  5. Step 5 of 5

    The money is already yours.

    The payment shows on your dashboard against Unit 4B, matched to the right tenant before she has used a drop.

Questions landlords ask before switching

Will my tenants accept prepaid water?
Most tenants already buy electricity tokens, so topping up water feels familiar. They pay only for what they use, see their usage on their phone, and we show them how it works when the meters go in.
Who pays for the meters?
It depends on the size of the building and the plan that suits you. After the site visit you get a fixed quote, so you know the full cost before anything is installed.
What happens when a tenant's credit runs out?
The water stops until they top up. Before that, they get an SMS when credit runs low, with time to top up from any phone.
Does every tenant need a smartphone?
No. Any phone with M-Pesa can pay through the paybill number, and the token arrives by SMS. The app is there for tenants who want to see their usage.
What if the network is down?
The tenant types the token from their SMS into the meter's keypad and the water flows as normal. The meter keeps its readings and sends them once the network is back.
Who sets the price of water?
You do, within the rules that apply in your area. Each building can have its own rate, and every top-up follows it.
Where does the money go?
Top-ups are paid through M-Pesa and settled to your account. Every payment is matched to a unit and a tenant, so you can see where each shilling came from.

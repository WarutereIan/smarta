# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-16

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Prepaid water for rental buildings. Tenants top up on M-Pesa, the landlord gets paid before the water is used.

**What it does:** Smarta fits a smart prepaid water meter to every unit. Tenants buy water the way they already buy electricity tokens. Landlords, managers and water providers see collections, usage, valves and leaks from one dashboard, and stop reading meters, sending bills and chasing arrears.

**Product category:** Prepaid water metering / water billing for landlords (how buyers search: "prepaid water meters Kenya", "water billing for apartments", "smart water meter landlord").

**Product type:** Hardware (STS prepaid meters from Stron with GSM and valve) + SaaS dashboard + tenant app + M-Pesa vending.

## Target Audience and segments

| Segment | Who decides | Job to be done | What they buy (lead message) | The ease they feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Landlords** (primary) | Owner of 1–5 blocks | Get paid for water without chasing anyone | "Never chase a water bill again." | Money arrives before water is used. Nothing to read, bill or collect. Move-outs settle themselves. |
| **Property managers / agencies** | Director, portfolio manager | Run more buildings with the same staff, keep landlords happy | "Manage more doors with the same team." | Statements build themselves. Cash stays out of caretakers' hands. A better offer to win landlords. |
| **Developers & estates** | Developer, estate management company | Hand over homes with utilities sorted; keep water out of service-charge fights | "Hand over homes with water already sorted." | Metered from day one; water never mixes into the service charge; a selling point at viewings. |
| **Water service providers / community water projects** | MD, commercial manager | Collect revenue and cut water lost before the tap | "Collect before the water leaves the reservoir." | Fewer field trips for readings and disconnections; loss per zone becomes a number. |
| **Tenants** (users, must be won over) | Each household | Pay only for my own water, no surprise bills | "Top up water like you top up electricity." | Familiar M-Pesa flow, low-credit SMS, usage on the phone, fair to the litre. |

Homepage leads with **landlords**; other segments get a tab on the homepage now and their own landing pages later.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem (landlord):** Postpaid water makes the landlord the building's debt collector. Water is used on day one; the cost is known weeks later; collecting it is awkward and often fails.

**What it costs them:** water arrears that never get paid, tenants who move out owing, caretaker time on readings, cash going missing, disputes over shared or estimated bills, leaks that show up as a bill.

**Emotional tension:** awkward conversations with tenants, feeling taken advantage of, not trusting the caretaker's cash or readings, paying the water company for water someone else used.

## Differentiation

- Works like electricity tokens, which tenants already understand.
- M-Pesa top-ups from any phone; token also by SMS for keypad entry.
- Remote valve: close a vacant unit from a phone.
- Leak and tamper alerts, not just billing.
- Installed and set up for you, starting with one building.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Who pays for the meters?" | Fixed quote after the site visit. [NEED: financing / no-upfront option] |
| "My tenants won't accept it." | They already buy electricity tokens; they pay only for their own use; we brief them at install. |
| "What if it breaks or the network fails?" | Token also arrives by SMS for keypad entry; meter stores readings offline. [NEED: support / warranty terms] |
| "Cutting water off feels harsh." | Low-credit SMS well before the valve closes. [NEED: emergency credit policy] |

## Switching dynamics

- **Push:** arrears, disputes, move-outs owing, reading costs, leaks.
- **Pull:** paid up front, no admin, fairness for tenants, phone control.
- **Habit:** "the caretaker handles it", existing postpaid meters.
- **Anxiety:** cost, tenant pushback, reliability. Answer with a one-building pilot and a fixed quote.

## Customer Language

**Words to use:** top up, token, M-Pesa, paybill, unit, block, caretaker, arrears, deposit, move out, meter reading, leak, water bill, Nairobi Water / county water bill.
**Words to avoid on marketing pages:** STS, IEC 62055-41, GSM/GPRS, DN20, dBm, telemetry, head-end, NRW (say "water lost before the tap"), model numbers of sensors. Keep these for spec sheets and the FAQ.

## Brand Voice

| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean |
|---|---|---|
| Plain | Words a landlord uses on the phone | Jargon, spec sheets |
| Practical | Concrete scenes: a locked gate, a running toilet at 3 a.m. | Vague promises |
| Calm | Problems are ordinary and fixable | Fear-mongering about losses |

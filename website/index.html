The postpaid month

On a postpaid meter, you're the last to know.

Water gets used on day one. You find out what it cost weeks later, after a reading, a bill and often an argument.

  1. Day 1

    Water runs in 24 units

    Nobody sees how much, or where.

  2. Day 30

    The caretaker walks the block

    Two dials are fogged. One meter sits behind a locked gate.

  3. Day 35

    Bills go out

    Unit 6 says the reading is wrong, and you have no record to show them.

  4. Day 60

    You chase the rest by phone

    Water arrears sit next to rent arrears, and rent wins.

  5. Day 61

    The leak arrives as a bill

    A toilet in Unit 3 has run for six weeks. Someone pays for that water.

Prepaid flips the order. Tenants pay first, the meter counts down, and you see every unit as it happens.

How a top-up works

From M-Pesa to a running tap.

One tenant, one KSh 500 top-up, followed from her phone to your dashboard. Scroll to run it.

  1. Step 1 of 5

    Wanjiru pays KSh 500 from her phone.

    She uses the Smarta app or the paybill number on her meter sticker. An M-Pesa prompt asks for her PIN, the same way she pays for anything else.

  2. Step 2 of 5

    Smarta issues a 20-digit STS token.

    The token is encrypted for her meter alone. It won't work on Unit 4C, and her own meter accepts it exactly once.

  3. Step 3 of 5

    The token reaches the meter over GSM.

    She also gets it by SMS. If the signal in the meter chamber is weak, she types the 20 digits on the keypad and the credit lands the same way.

  4. Step 4 of 5

    The meter adds 5.0 m³ and opens the valve.

    Credit counts down as she uses water. When it runs low she gets an SMS, well before the valve closes.

  5. Step 5 of 5

    Your dashboard updates.

    Payment, token, credit and consumption are recorded against Unit 4B, already reconciled. The money is in your account before a drop is used.

The meter

Built for a flooded meter chamber and five years without a battery change.

Each Smarta meter is a prepaid STS water meter from Stron with its own GSM module, valve and memory. Select a part to see what it does for you.

  • Motorised valve

    Close a vacant unit or a leaking line from your phone. The meter reports the valve position back, so you know it actually closed.

  • Tamper detection

    Magnets held against the meter, an opened cover or reversed flow raise an alert with the unit, the time and the type of attempt.

  • GSM / GPRS module

    Readings, alarms and tokens travel over the mobile network. There is no gateway to install or power in the building.

  • Offline memory

    When the network drops, the meter keeps logging. It uploads the backlog when it reconnects, so your history has no gaps.

  • Five-year battery

    Sized for five years or more in the field. Battery level shows on your dashboard long before a replacement is due.

  • IP67 enclosure

    Sealed against dust and temporary immersion, for meter chambers that fill with water in the long rains.

  • Over-the-air updates

    Firmware updates go out in batches, with a record of which meters installed them and which need a retry.

  • Real-time flow rate

    A toilet running at 3 a.m. looks different from a shower at 7. Flow rate is how Smarta tells them apart.

Questions landlords ask

Before you meter a building

Something else on your mind? Ask during the pilot call.

What happens when a tenant's credit runs out?
The valve closes until they top up. Before that, the tenant gets an SMS when credit falls below the warning level you set for the building.
Does every tenant need a smartphone?
No. Any phone with M-Pesa can pay through the paybill number, and the token arrives by SMS. The app is there for tenants who want usage history and reminders.
What if the meter loses signal?
The tenant types the 20-digit token from their SMS on the meter keypad. The meter keeps storing readings and events and sends them when the signal returns.
Who sets the price of water?
You do, within the rules that apply in your area. Each building can have a flat rate per cubic metre or tiered rates, and the token value follows the tariff.
Can tenants bypass or tamper with the meter?
Tamper attempts raise an alert on your dashboard. Each STS token works only on the meter it was issued for, and only once, so tokens can't be shared or reused.
Which meters do you install?
STS prepaid water meters from Stron, with a built-in GSM/GPRS module, a motorised valve, an IP67 enclosure and a battery rated for five years or more.
What does a pilot involve?
We start with one building: a site survey, meter installation, tenant onboarding and a month of reporting. Then you decide on the rest of your portfolio with your own data.
